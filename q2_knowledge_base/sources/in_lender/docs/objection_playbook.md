# Objection Handling Playbook – Pre-Due Reminder Calls
Owner: Collections Training Team · Last reviewed: 20-Aug-2026

## Objection: "Why are you calling me before the due date?"
Thank the customer for their repayment track record. Explain that this is a courtesy reminder so that the instalment is not missed and a bounce charge of ₹590 (incl. GST) plus their own bank's charges are avoided. Offer to skip further reminders by confirming auto-debit balance.

## Objection: "I don't have money right now / salary is late"
Acknowledge the situation. Ask when the customer expects funds. If the expected date is within the grace period of 5 days after the due date, record a promise to pay. If later, explain that late payment charges and credit bureau reporting may apply and offer a callback from the Customer Assistance team.

## Objection: "I already paid"
Thank the customer. Ask for the payment date, mode and UTR / receipt number. Explain that payments can take up to 2 working days to reflect and raise a payment trace request. Never argue with the customer.

## Objection: "Waive my late fee / bounce charge"
Explain politely that reminder agents cannot waive or reduce charges. The request can be reviewed by the Customer Assistance team within 5 working days. Do not promise an outcome.

## Objection: "Stop calling me"
Apologise for the inconvenience. Explain that this is a one-time pre-due reminder. Offer to set up auto-debit (NACH) so manual reminders are not needed. Respect a request not to be called again during the current cycle and record it.

## Objection: "Is this call a fraud? How do I know you are from Udaan?"
Never ask for OTP, PIN or card details. Tell the customer they can verify by calling the toll-free number 1800-000-1234 or by checking the loan details in the Udaan app. Offer to end the call and let them pay through the app.

## Objection: "I want to close my loan instead"
Share that personal loans can be foreclosed after 6 EMIs and foreclosure charges apply as per the schedule of charges. Offer to request a foreclosure statement through the app or customer care.
