Branch Partnership Program

Branch Partnership Benefits

Operational, marketing, and technology support is provided to branch partners. Partners receive a co-branded outlet kit, access to the Udaan partner CRM, a dedicated relationship manager and monthly payout on disbursed volumes.

Who can apply

Existing DSA agencies, insurance POSPs and retail finance outlets with at least 2 years of operating history, a registered GST number and a clean credit record of the proprietor.

Payout structure

Payout is 1.0% to 1.75% of disbursed loan amount depending on monthly volume slabs, credited by the 10th of the following month.
