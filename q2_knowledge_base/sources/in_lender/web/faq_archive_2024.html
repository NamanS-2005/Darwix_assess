Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 02/08/2024

What happens if I miss my EMI due date?

If the EMI is not received within the grace period, a late payment charge of 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount is levied, along with a bounce charge of ₹500 plus GST if an auto-debit or cheque fails. Missed payments are reported to credit bureaus such as CIBIL and may lower your credit score.

Will a late payment affect my CIBIL score?

Yes. Udaan reports repayment status to all four credit bureaus every month. A payment delayed beyond the due date can be reported as overdue (DPD) and may reduce your credit score. Paying within the grace period avoids late charges, but the bureau status depends on whether the EMI was paid before the reporting date.

Can I change my instalment date?

Yes, once during the loan tenure. Submit the request at least 7 days before the next due date. Available dates are the 5th and 10th.

I have already paid but still received a reminder. What should I do?

Payments can take up to 2 working days to reflect. If you paid through UPI, net banking or branch, keep your UTR or receipt number ready and share it with us. We will trace the payment and update your account. You will not be charged a late fee for a payment that was made on or before the due date.
