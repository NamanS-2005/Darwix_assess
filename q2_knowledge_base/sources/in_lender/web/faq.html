Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 12 March 2026

What happens if I miss my EMI due date?

If the EMI is not received within the grace period, a late payment charge of 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount is levied, along with a bounce charge of ₹590 (inclusive of GST) if an auto-debit or cheque fails. Missed payments are reported to credit bureaus such as CIBIL and may lower your credit score.

Will a late payment affect my CIBIL score?

Yes. Udaan reports repayment status to all four credit bureaus every month. A payment delayed beyond the due date can be reported as overdue (DPD) and may reduce your credit score. Paying within the grace period avoids late charges, but the bureau status depends on whether the EMI was paid before the reporting date.

Can I change my EMI due date?

Yes, once during the loan tenure. Submit the EMI Date Change Request form at least 10 days before the next due date. Available dates are the 2nd, 5th, 10th and 15th. Broken-period interest for the extra days is added to the next EMI.

I have already paid but still received a reminder. What should I do?

Payments can take up to 2 working days to reflect. If you paid through UPI, net banking or branch, keep your UTR or receipt number ready and share it with us. We will trace the payment and update your account. You will not be charged a late fee for a payment that was made on or before the due date.

Can I pay part of my EMI?

Part payment of an EMI is accepted but the unpaid balance remains overdue and attracts late payment charges. We recommend paying the full EMI before the due date.

I am facing financial difficulty. What are my options?

Please tell us early. Depending on eligibility, our Customer Assistance team can offer a one-time EMI date shift, a short payment plan for the overdue amount, or tenure restructuring as per our board-approved policy. These are reviewed case by case by a specialist and are not guaranteed.

Can I foreclose or prepay my loan?

Personal loans can be foreclosed after 6 EMIs are paid. The foreclosure charge is 4% of principal outstanding plus GST. There is no foreclosure charge for individual borrowers on floating-rate loans. Part-prepayment of up to 25% of principal outstanding is allowed once a year.

How do I get my loan statement or interest certificate?

Download it from the Udaan app under My Loans > Documents, or request it by email from your registered email ID. Statements are free once a year; additional copies cost ₹200 plus GST.

Is my EMI amount going to change?

For fixed-rate loans the EMI stays the same for the full tenure. For floating-rate loans the EMI or tenure may change when the benchmark rate changes; you will be informed in advance.
